## What this does

Aspect ratio is the shape of the frame. 16:9 is the wide frame used for most film and television layouts. 9:16 is the tall frame used for vertical delivery. Storyboard Studio offers those two.

## Why you choose it early

A wide composition and a tall composition are different shots. A face that works in 16:9 can fall out of a 9:16 frame, and the reverse is true for a landscape. Generate the video in the shape you boarded, or expect to crop a composition that was never designed for the other shape.

## How it works

Select 16:9 or 9:16 on the board. If you change it, the panels stay linked. You are not forked into two unrelated boards by the switch. You are looking at the same plan in the other shape, so check the crop before you trust it.

## What to expect

Video models have their own resolution and aspect support. The board's aspect is your intent. The model still has to accept a matching output. If a model cannot produce that shape, the generation should say so rather than silently giving you a different frame.

## Common mistakes

Boarding in 16:9 and delivering 9:16 by cropping the middle later, then wondering why the character's hands are gone. If both deliveries matter, plan both frames.
