## Verdict

**NOT READY FOR MANUAL USER BETA**

> **Superseded 2026-07-27, verdict unchanged.** B1-B4 and B10 are closed and real local and
> fal artifacts now exist inside the product, but the tally is still **0 EXECUTED / 12

The reasoning is short. A manual beta user cannot produce a single frame or a single second
of audio, because no generative provider exists for any medium. That alone would justify
"with conditions" rather than "not ready" - a beta can be scoped around missing capability.
What moves this to "not ready" is that the system does not consistently say so. The image
and video endpoints return HTTP 200 with a populated `assetId`, `fixture: false` and
`status: "draft"` for artifacts that were never created and have no database row and no
file. The workspace where the Storyteller and Sound Producer journeys live cannot render in
the browser at all. The mix-revise step of the required sound path accepts a request,
returns `status: "processed"`, and does nothing.

Those are not gaps a beta user can work around or report usefully. They would produce
feedback about a product that does not exist.

M3.0a asked M3.0b not to pretend live productions succeeded if they were not run. They were
not run, and this report does not pretend otherwise.

### Update - B1, B2 and B3 fixed in the working tree, verdict unchanged

After the audit above was written, the three honesty defects it names were fixed as

The verdict is still **NOT READY FOR MANUAL USER BETA**, and the paragraphs above still read

"With conditions" would require the remaining gaps to be scoped provider-absence conditions a

would see it. Both are on this report's own minimum bar. Until they are closed, the honest
answer is still no.

`mayMutateBible: false` and `mayMutateTimeline: false` are enforced rather than advisory;
across twelve consecutive real runs nothing was written that had not been approved.

could reach firm conclusions rather than guesses.

**Confusing.** `status: "draft"` with a populated `assetId` and `fixture: false` reads as

## Sound and score dedicated path

**Status: PARTIAL.** Executed in the production configuration; fifteen of sixteen hops
returned HTTP 200.

Open scene, Storyteller emotional arc, handoff and approval, Sound Producer sonic plan and
approval, Cinematographer timing message, Editor timing message, score, ambience, SFX, cue
persistence, timeline, preview, mix revise, export - all reachable. What the HTTP 200s mean
varies sharply:

- **Real and correct:** scene open, handoff and approval mechanics, specialist messaging,
  audio plan validation, cue persistence, export enqueue.
- **Structure real, content constant:** Storyteller arc and Sound Producer plan, both
  labelled `honesty: "unavailable"`, both identical across all twelve situations.
- **No audio at all:** score, ambience and SFX each returned `providerMissing: true` with
  the cue left as `draft` and `assetId: null`.
- **Two defects rather than absences:** generated cues never reach the director timeline
  (`audio_clips: []`), and mix revise is a silent no-op because the HTTP contract types
  `ops` as `list[dict]` while the executor matches on `list[str]` - the two shapes are
  mutually exclusive, so loudness normalisation is unreachable through the API.

One real artifact was produced during this milestone and it is worth naming precisely
because it is the only one: ffmpeg loudness normalisation on a supplied WAV stem produced a
288,078-byte output resampled from 44,100 Hz to 48,000 Hz. That is real media
**processing**, reached through the internal executor. It is not generation and it does not
realise any situation.

## Job queue restart recovery: FIXED

This was an open M3.0a condition (`RST = FAIL` across queue-backed platforms) and it is
closed in code.

`JobQueue.recover_interrupted()` in `studio-api/app/queue_worker.py` runs from the FastAPI
lifespan in `studio-api/app/main.py` before the worker starts. It re-enqueues recent
`queued` jobs so work resumes; marks `running` jobs `failed` with stage `interrupted` and a
message saying the work was interrupted by a restart rather than that it failed on its own
merits, because a provider-side render held by a dead process cannot be resumed by a new
one; marks `queued` jobs older than `STUDIO_JOB_RECOVERY_MAX_AGE_HOURS` as interrupted
rather than starting stale work; leaves `done`, `failed` and `cancelled` untouched; and
records every action in the job's history for audit.

`studio-api/tests/test_job_queue_recovery.py` adds seven tests covering resume, honest
interruption, the staleness cutoff, terminal-job safety, the history audit trail,
configurability, and the API startup hook. All seven pass.

Verified not to disturb the rest of the suite: the twenty pre-existing pytest failures are
identical with and without this test file present.

This should also be recorded in the M3.0a remediation log as the closure of that condition.
